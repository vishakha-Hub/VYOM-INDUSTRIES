Our Products
Vyom Industries offers a diverse range of high-quality electrical distribution boards and control panels designed for both industrial and residential use. Our products include:
Power Distribution Boards: Ensuring reliable and safe distribution of electricity, our power distribution boards are designed for high performance and long-lasting durability.


Electrical Distribution Boards: Built to handle electrical systems with efficiency and reliability, offering protection and control for a wide variety of applications.


Legrand Distribution Boards: These premium boards, featuring advanced technology, provide superior performance and safety, ideal for commercial and industrial environments.


Distribution Panel Boards: Designed for optimal power distribution, our panel boards offer both versatility and robust functionality, meeting the demands of complex systems.


MCB Distribution Boards: Miniature Circuit Breaker (MCB) distribution boards provide essential protection against overcurrents and short circuits, ensuring the safety of electrical circuits.


TPN Distribution Boards: Three-Phase Neutral (TPN) distribution boards are designed for high-efficiency applications, providing uninterrupted power flow for industrial operations.


Legrand MCB Distribution Boards: A combination of Legrand’s trusted technology and MCB safety features, these boards ensure maximum reliability and protection in electrical systems.


Electric Control Panels: Vyom Industries designs and manufactures efficient electric control panels that are essential for the automated operation of machinery and industrial systems.


Control Panels: Our control panels are designed for various industrial and commercial purposes, ensuring smooth operations, efficient energy use, and enhanced safety.
