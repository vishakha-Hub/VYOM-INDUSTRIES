About Us

ELECTRICAL
EXCELLENCE

Founded in 2018, Vyom Industries is a forward-thinking company specializing in the manufacturing and supply of high-quality electrical distribution products. We cater to diverse industries by offering reliable, innovative, and durable solutions for efficient power management.

With a commitment to safety and precision, Vyom Industries is the partner you can trust for all your electrical distribution needs — from residential setups to large-scale industrial installations.

Our Vision

To be a global leader in providing reliable, innovative, and sustainable electrical distribution solutions, empowering industries with the highest standards of safety, efficiency, and performance.

Our Mission

To manufacture and deliver top-notch electrical distribution products, invest in research and innovation, provide exceptional customer service, and foster a culture of sustainability and integrity.
